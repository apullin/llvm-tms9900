# Primary-Source Vat Index (What Was Distilled)

This manifest catalogs the ~500K-token corpus of hardware manuals, TI-99/4A books, console ROM binaries, compiler journals, and production assembly code from which `CORE_CONSTITUTION.md`, `cards/*.md`, and `exemplars/*.S` were precipitated.

|---|---|---:|---|

| **TI-99/4A Editor/Assembler Manual** | `../books/editor-assembler manual.pdf` | 473 pp (~165K tok) | Section 24.3 Scratchpad PAD `0x8300–0x83FF` map, `KSCAN` modes & `0x8374..0x837C`, `GPLLNK`/`XMLLNK`/`DSRLNK` contracts, VDP RAM layouts, CRU `0x0000–0x1FFE` & TMS9901 pinout, EA5 binary loader format. |
| **Compute!'s Beginner's Guide to Assembly Language on the TI-99/4A (Lottrup)** | `../books/computes-beginners-guide-to-assembly-language-on-the-ti994a_djvu.html` | ~105K tok | Practical sound/sprite/keyboard patterns; exposed the trap that `GPLLNK` at `0x6018` requires the E/A cartridge and is absent in standalone carts. |
